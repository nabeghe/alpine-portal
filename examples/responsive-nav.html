🧭 Responsive Navigation

The navigation links teleport from the mobile drawer to the desktop header bar at 768px. On mobile, they live inside a slide-out drawer triggered by the hamburger button. On desktop, they sit inline in the header — no duplication needed.

📱 Mobile (< 768px)

Links live inside the drawer. Tap the hamburger ☰ icon to open it.

💻 Desktop (≥ 768px)

Links teleport to the header bar and appear as a horizontal menu.

🔄 Bidirectional

Resize back and forth — the links always end up in the right place.

💡 How it works: <ul x-portal="#desktop-nav" x-portal:screen="768"> — the nav links are defined inside the mobile drawer and teleported to #desktop-nav in the header when viewport ≥ 768px.
